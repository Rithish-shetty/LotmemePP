ARTICLE 001
Who We Are

ChaosMart is a parody card generator app that lets users create silly, satirical cards including fake tabloids, diplomas, wanted posters, receipts, roast certificates, and more. This app is built for entertainment purposes only.

ChaosMart uses Google Sign-In (OAuth) for authentication. We never store or see your Google password. We only receive your email address and basic profile info from Google to create your account.

OFFICIAL DECLARATION

ChaosMart is operated as an independent app by Nexias Dev. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at nexiasdev@gmail.com.

ARTICLE 002
Information We Collect

We collect only what we absolutely need. Here is the complete list — no hidden surprises:

Data Type What It Is Why We Collect It
Google Account Email Your email address from Google Sign-In To create and identify your ChaosMart account
Google Profile Name Your display name from your Google account To personalise your experience in the app
Subscription Status Whether you are a Pro or free user To unlock Pro features and remove ads
Username (local only) The name you set inside the app Stored only on your device — never sent to our servers
Photos (optional, local only) Images you upload for cards Used within the app only — never uploaded to our servers
WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do not collect your Google password, phone number, location, contacts, browsing history, device files, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above. Card content you generate is never stored on our servers.

ARTICLE 003
How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your ChaosMart account using Google Sign-In
  • To verify your subscription status and unlock Pro features
  • To process in-app purchases via RevenueCat (our subscription provider)
  • To serve advertisements to free users via Google AdMob
  • To generate AI-powered card content via Google Gemini API — your input text is sent to Gemini to generate results but is not stored by us
OUR PROMISE

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for marketing. We do not share your data with anyone except the essential third-party services listed in Article 004 that are required to operate the app.

ARTICLE 005
Data Storage & Security

Your account data (email + subscription status) is stored securely on Supabase servers with row-level security enabled. This means only you can access your own data.

  • We never store your Google password — authentication is handled entirely by Google
  • All connections to our servers use HTTPS encryption
  • Your username stays on your device only — it is never sent to our servers
  • Photos you upload for cards are processed locally on your device — they are never sent to or stored on our servers
  • Card content, generated text, and creative output never leaves your device
WHAT WE NEVER SEE

Your Google password, card content, generated headlines, roast text, wanted poster descriptions, photos, and any other creative output. We never have access to any of it.
